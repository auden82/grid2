COOKIES POLICY

Last updated: 3 August 2026

ABOUT THIS POLICY

This policy explains how grid2 uses cookies and similar technologies on grid2.me. It also explains the technologies that may be used by third-party services available through our website.

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, may perform related functions. They can help a website operate, remember information or understand how it is used.

HOW WE USE COOKIES

grid2 does not currently use its own analytics, advertising or personalisation cookies on this website. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages, and its core pages do not require you to create an account or sign in.

Our contact form uses technology supplied by Brevo. We also link to payment pages operated by Stripe. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to deliver, secure and protect their services.

No optional cookie banner is currently shown because grid2 does not intentionally place non-essential cookies on the site. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other optional cookies, we will update this policy and provide an appropriate consent choice before those technologies are activated.

SERVICES USED ON OR FROM THIS WEBSITE

Service When it applies Purpose Provider
Website delivery When you visit grid2.me To host and securely deliver the website. grid2 does not use this service to set analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages
Contact form When the contact form and its supporting files load, or when you submit it To operate the form, process your submission, prevent misuse and maintain security. Brevo
Payment page After you select a price and leave grid2.me for a Stripe checkout page To provide and secure checkout, prevent fraud and process payment. Stripe's own policies apply on its website. Stripe

Third-party cookie names and retention periods may be changed by those providers. You can review the Brevo privacy policy and Stripe privacy policy for further information.

MANAGING COOKIES

You can use your browser settings to view, block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect the operation of some website features, including forms or payment services.

Browser controls vary. Look for “Privacy”, “Cookies” or “Site data” in your browser settings for the relevant options. If grid2 later introduces optional cookies, you will also be able to manage them through the choices provided on this website.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Cookies Policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

CONTACT

If you have a question about this policy or the technologies used on this website, contact us at contact@grid2.me.
