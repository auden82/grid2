YOUR INFORMATION

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information grid2 collects, why it is used, who it may be shared with and the choices you have. We only use personal information where we have a lawful reason to do so.

1. WHO WE ARE

grid2 provides marketing services and is the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email contact@grid2.me.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • your name, business name, job title and contact details;
  • information you include in an enquiry, project brief or correspondence;
  • service, quotation, contract, invoice and transaction information;
  • content, files or account access you provide for a marketing project;
  • technical information such as IP address, browser type and basic website logs; and
  • marketing preferences and records of consent, where applicable.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and we have agreed how it will be handled.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Enquiries and service delivery

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, enter into and manage contracts, deliver services, communicate about projects and provide support. The lawful bases are taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the business.

Payments, records and legal duties

We use transaction and business records to process payments, manage accounts, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations. The lawful bases are contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests.

Marketing

We may send marketing where you have consented or where the law otherwise permits it. You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe option in a message or by emailing us. We will still send necessary service communications.

Website operation and security

We may use limited technical information to keep the website secure, diagnose faults and understand basic performance. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining a safe and effective website.

4. CONTACT FORMS, BREVO AND EMAIL

When you use our contact form, the information you enter is transmitted and stored using Brevo, which provides form and email services. Brevo processes that information on our behalf. You can also contact us directly by email.

Do not submit information through the form that you would not be comfortable sending by ordinary email.

5. PAYMENTS AND STRIPE

If you choose a paid service through a Stripe payment link or checkout page, Stripe collects and processes payment details under its own privacy terms. grid2 does not receive your full card number. We may receive transaction details such as your name, email address, billing information, payment status and amount paid so we can fulfil the purchase and keep appropriate records.

6. COOKIES

The website may use essential cookies or similar technology needed for security and functionality. Embedded or linked services such as Brevo and Stripe may set their own cookies when you interact with them. See our Cookies Policy for more information.

7. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

We may share information only where reasonably necessary with:

  • service providers supporting email, forms, payment processing, website hosting, file storage, accounting or project delivery;
  • professional advisers such as accountants, insurers or legal advisers;
  • authorities, regulators or law-enforcement bodies where required by law; and
  • a buyer or successor if the business is sold or reorganised, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we require an appropriate legal safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections, unless another lawful exception applies.

9. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including to provide services and meet legal, accounting or dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Unsuccessful or one-off enquiries: normally up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client and project records: normally for the relationship and up to 6 years afterwards.
  • Financial and tax records: normally 6 years after the relevant financial period, or longer where legally required.
  • Marketing records: until you opt out or the information is no longer useful, with a minimal suppression record retained to respect your preference.

We may keep information longer if a legal claim, investigation or other legal requirement makes this necessary. Information may be deleted sooner when it is no longer required.

10. SECURITY

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration and disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you the right to:

  • ask for access to your personal information;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to erase or restrict the use of information;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.

These rights are not absolute and legal exceptions may apply. To make a request, email contact@grid2.me. We may need to verify your identity.

12. COMPLAINTS

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK data-protection regulator, through ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy when our services, suppliers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
