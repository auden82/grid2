FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I have to order before contacting grid2?

No. You are welcome to contact us first. We can understand your request, answer questions and advise on the most suitable service before you place an order.

What happens after I place an order?

We will contact you to confirm the requirements, scope, required information and expected timescale before the work begins.

Can I combine more than one service?

Yes. Services can be combined where a project needs support across several areas, such as strategy, content, social media and campaigns.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. grid2 supports small businesses and growing companies that need practical, straightforward marketing support.

Is ongoing support available?

Yes. You can choose an individual service for a specific project or use Monthly Support when you need regular marketing help.
